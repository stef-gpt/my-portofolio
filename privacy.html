In short

This Site is a portfolio. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking tools, and it does not set cookies of its own. The sections below explain the small number of cases where information moves.

What the Site collects

The Site does not collect personal information through its pages. The contact form is not connected yet, so anything you type into it stays in your browser and is not saved or sent anywhere by the Site.

If you email me, I receive your email address and your message. I use them only to reply to you.

Hosting and server logs

The Site is served by a hosting provider. Like most hosts, it may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. These logs are used for security and reliability, not to identify visitors.

Cookies and storage

The Site does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Third party services

Links to GitHub, Instagram and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies. I am not responsible for how they handle your data. Fonts or media loaded from outside services, if any, may receive your IP address when they load.

Keeping and deleting your information

Emails are kept only as long as needed to reply and keep a reasonable record. To ask me to delete a conversation, write to stefanigabriella012@gmail.com and I will remove it.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
